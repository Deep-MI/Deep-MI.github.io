Privacy Policy

How this website handles personal data.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Dr. Martin Reuter (contact details: see the Legal Notice). Please use these contact details for any privacy-related questions.

2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub. When you access the site, your browser automatically transmits technical data to GitHub's servers, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested page, the referring URL and information about your browser and device. GitHub needs this data to deliver the website. According to GitHub, the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes. We do not have access to these logs.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the reliable provision of this website. Data may be transferred to the USA. For details, including the safeguards for such transfers, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection.

3. Embedded YouTube videos

Some pages offer videos hosted on YouTube, a service of Google. These videos are not loaded automatically; a preview image is shown instead. Only when you click to play a video is it loaded from YouTube. At that point your IP address and other data are transmitted to Google, and data may be transferred to the USA. The legal basis is your consent by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). For details, see Google's privacy policy. Alternatively, you can follow the link to watch the video directly on YouTube.

Your consent applies only to that single click and is not stored: the video is not loaded again on a later visit unless you click again. You can therefore withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR) simply by not clicking again.

4. Contact by e-mail

If you e-mail us, we process your message and the data it contains in order to reply, and keep it as part of our regular correspondence. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in communicating with you).

5. External links

This website contains links to external websites (e.g. GitHub, publishers, social media). Data is only transmitted to these providers when you follow a link. Their own privacy policies apply.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21).

We do not collect or store any data about visitors to this website ourselves. These rights therefore concern us mainly if you have contacted us (section 4). For data processed by GitHub as host or by Google when you play a video, please refer to their privacy policies linked above.

If you have any questions or concerns about how your data is handled, please contact us first (see section 1). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
